MEET YOUR HOST

Explore New York with an experienced local guide.

Makan Camara has more than 17 years of experience as a pedicab driver in New York City. He knows Central Park and Midtown Manhattan intimately and enjoys bringing their landmarks, history, and hidden stories to life for visitors from around the world.

Makan speaks English, French, and Spanish, helping guests feel comfortable and welcome throughout their experience. He is also president of the New York Pedicab Alliance and believes every visitor deserves a safe, friendly tour with clear prices and honest service.
